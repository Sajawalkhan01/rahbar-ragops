# Library Borrowing Rules


## Borrowing Limits

An undergraduate student may borrow up to four books at a time for a period of fourteen days.   Postgraduate students may borrow up to six books for twenty-one days. Reference books, past papers and journals cannot be taken out of the library.

## Renewal and Fines

A loan can be renewed twice if no other member has reserved the book.   A fine of Rs 20 per day is charged for every overdue book. A student with unpaid library fines cannot collect a transcript or register for the next semester.

## Lost Books

A lost or damaged book must be replaced with the same edition, or the student pays the current price of the book plus a processing charge of Rs 500.
