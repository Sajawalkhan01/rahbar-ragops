# Notice on Make-up Examinations

A student who misses a sessional examination because of illness must submit a medical certificate to the instructor within three working days. The instructor may allow one make-up examination per course per semester. A make-up examination for the final paper is allowed only with the written permission of the Dean.

Students who miss an examination without a valid reason are not entitled to a make-up examination, and zero marks are recorded for the missed assessment. The make-up examination covers the same topics as the missed one and is held within two weeks of the original date.
