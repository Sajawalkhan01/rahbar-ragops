# Merit and Need-Based Scholarship Policy

## Merit Scholarship Eligibility

A student qualifies for the merit scholarship if the semester GPA is 3.50 or higher and the student has registered for at least 15 credit hours in that semester. Students who hold an academic warning in the same semester are not eligible for the merit scholarship. The scholarship covers 50% of the tuition fee for the following semester.

## Need-Based Financial Aid

Need-based aid is awarded after a review by the Financial Aid Committee. An applicant must submit an income certificate, the last three months of utility bills and a copy of the family tax return. Aid is limited to a maximum of 75% of the tuition fee and is reviewed every semester.

## Scholarship Renewal

A scholarship is renewed automatically when the recipient maintains a CGPA of at least 3.00 and has no disciplinary record. A recipient whose CGPA falls below 3.00 for one semester is placed on probation for the next semester. The scholarship is withdrawn if the CGPA stays below 3.00 for two consecutive semesters.

## Application Deadline

Applications for the Fall semester must reach the Financial Aid Office by the end of the second week of July. Late applications are considered only when funds remain available.
