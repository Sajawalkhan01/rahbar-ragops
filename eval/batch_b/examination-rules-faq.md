# Examination Rules FAQ

## Reporting Time

Students must be seated in the examination hall at least ten minutes before the paper starts. A student who arrives after the first thirty minutes of a final examination is not allowed to sit the paper.

## Items Allowed in the Hall

Only a calculator without programmable memory, a pen and the admit card are allowed. Mobile phones, smart watches and written notes must be left outside the hall. Possession of a mobile phone in the hall is treated as unfair means.

## Unfair Means

A student found using unfair means in a sessional or final examination receives zero marks in that course and a disciplinary notice. A second offence leads to suspension for one semester.

## Result Rechecking

A student may apply for rechecking of a final paper within three working days of the result announcement by paying the rechecking fee. The marks after rechecking are final.
