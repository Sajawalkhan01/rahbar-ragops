# Undergraduate Academic Rules

## Attendance

A student must attend at least 80% of the lectures of a course to be allowed to sit the final examination. Students with attendance below 80% are awarded the grade F in the course.

## Academic Warning

A student whose CGPA falls below 1.80 at the end of a semester is placed on academic warning for the next semester.
