# Library Lending Regulations

## How Many Books Can Be Taken

Every bachelor's student is entitled to hold four titles simultaneously, each for two weeks. Master's and doctoral students may hold six titles for three weeks. Journals, reference works and old examination papers must stay inside the library.

## Extensions and Penalties

Borrowers can extend a loan two times, unless somebody else has put a hold on it. Twenty rupees per day is charged as a penalty on each late title. Anyone owing library penalties is blocked from receiving a transcript or enrolling in the next term.

## Damaged or Missing Items

If a title is lost or damaged the borrower must supply the identical edition, or pay its present price together with a Rs 500 handling charge.
