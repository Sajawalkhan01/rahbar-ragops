# Course Withdrawal Guide

## Withdrawing From a Course

A student may withdraw from a course up to the end of the ninth week of the semester. A withdrawn course appears on the transcript with the grade W and is not counted in the CGPA. A student may withdraw from at most four courses during the whole degree program.

## Minimum Load After Withdrawal

After a withdrawal the student must still be registered for at least nine credit hours in the semester, unless the student is in the final semester of the degree.

## Semester Freeze

A semester may be frozen within the first two weeks of the semester. A frozen semester does not count toward the maximum duration of the degree. The freeze fee is paid at the time of the request and is not refundable.
