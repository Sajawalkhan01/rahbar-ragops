# Annual Sports Gala

## Events

The annual sports gala includes cricket, football, badminton and table tennis. Each department may enter one team per event. A student who plays for a department team must register with the sports committee before the first match.

## Schedule and Prizes

The matches start at ten in the morning and the finals are held on the last Friday of the gala week. The winning team of each event receives a trophy and the best player of the tournament receives a certificate. Students must wear sports shoes and carry the student card on the ground.
