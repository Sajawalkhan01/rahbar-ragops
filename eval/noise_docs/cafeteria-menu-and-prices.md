# Cafeteria Menu and Prices

## Lunch Menu

Students can buy a lunch meal of rice and curry for Rs 350 or a chicken burger for Rs 400. A cup of tea costs Rs 60 and a bottle of water costs Rs 80. The cafeteria is open from eight in the morning until six in the evening on all working days.

## Payment

Payment can be made in cash or with the student card. A student who loses the card must report it to the cafeteria manager. The fee for a replacement card is Rs 500 and the card can be collected after three working days.
