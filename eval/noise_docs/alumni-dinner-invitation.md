# Alumni Reunion Dinner

## Invitation

All graduates of the university are invited to the annual reunion dinner. The dinner will be held in the main auditorium on the first Saturday of December at seven in the evening. Graduates may bring one guest.

## Registration

Alumni must register by the end of November and pay a dinner fee of Rs 2,500 per person. The fee covers the dinner and a souvenir. Registration is not refundable after the deadline, and seats are allotted in the order of registration.
