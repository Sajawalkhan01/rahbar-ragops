# Campus Wi-Fi Guide

## Connecting

Students connect to the campus wireless network by selecting the student network and entering the university email address and password. A device must be registered with the IT office before it can connect. A student may register at most three devices.

## Problems

If the connection is slow, students should move closer to an access point or restart the device. Faults must be reported to the IT help desk, which is open on all working days from nine to five. Streaming services are limited during the working hours of the day.
